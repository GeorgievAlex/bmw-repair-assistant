E90 325i · N52B25

Engine · E90 · N52B25

Serpentine belt replacement

Replace the accessory belt that drives the alternator, water pump, power steering pump, and AC compressor off the front of the engine.

TorqueTensioner pulley mounting bolt: verify against RealOEM or dealer spec for your exact engine revision before relying on a memorized number, this is not a high-stakes fastener in terms of failure mode but it's still worth getting right.
PartsSerpentine belt and tensioner/idler pulleys: verify exact OEM part numbers against RealOEM.com using your VIN, there are revisions across the production run of this engine.
ToolsTensioner release tool or a long breaker bar, flashlight to see the routing clearly.
Time45-60 minutes
DifficultyMedium

Steps

Preparation

1. Note the belt routing before removing anything, either photograph it or check the routing diagram sticker under the hood, you'll need it to install the new one correctly.

Removal

  1. Use a breaker bar or a dedicated tensioner tool on the tensioner pulley to release belt tension.
  2. Slip the belt off the pulleys, starting with whichever is easiest to reach.
  3. While it's off, spin the tensioner and idler pulleys by hand, feel for roughness or play, replace them if anything feels off, they're cheap compared to a belt failure later.

Installation

  1. Route the new belt exactly per the diagram, the last pulley you route it onto is usually the alternator or AC compressor.
  2. Release the tensioner slowly and confirm the belt has seated properly in every pulley groove before starting the engine.

Notes

A mis-routed belt can spin the AC compressor or alternator backward, which is not something you want to discover after you've already started the engine. Double check the routing diagram before you pull the old belt off, not after.
