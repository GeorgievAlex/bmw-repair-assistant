# Engine oil and filter change

## Category
Maintenance

## Summary
Replace engine oil and the oil filter element as part of routine service.

## Steps
### Preparation
1. Warm the engine for a few minutes so the oil flows freely, then shut it off.
2. Remove the engine undertray.

### Draining
1. Remove the drain plug and let the oil fully drain into a pan, this takes longer than people expect, don't rush it.
2. Remove the oil filter housing cap (large plastic cap near the front of the engine), replace the filter element and both O-rings on the cap.

### Refilling
1. Reinstall the drain plug with a new crush washer, and the filter cap.
2. Refill with the correct oil spec and quantity, start the engine, check for leaks around the drain plug and filter cap, then check the level once settled.

## Torque Specs
Oil drain plug: around 25 Nm. Oil filter housing cap: around 25 Nm. Verify against RealOEM or dealer spec for your exact year, these are commonly cited figures, not pulled from your specific car's documentation.

## Part Numbers
Oil filter element and drain plug crush washer: verify exact OEM part numbers against RealOEM.com using your VIN. Oil spec: BMW Longlife-01 5W-30 is the commonly cited spec for this engine generation, capacity is roughly 6.5 liters with filter, confirm both against your owner's manual.

## Tools
Oil filter housing wrench (typically 36mm), drain pan, torque wrench.

## Difficulty
Easy

## Time Estimate
30-45 minutes

## Notes
Don't overtighten the filter housing cap, it's plastic and cracks easily, especially once it's a few years old. Let the oil drain fully before refilling, a partial drain leaves old oil and contaminants behind.
