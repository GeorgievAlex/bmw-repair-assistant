# Power steering fluid change

## Category
Steering

## Summary
Flush and replace the hydraulic power steering fluid. Old fluid turns dark and abrasive and is hard on the pump and rack seals.

## Steps
### Preparation
1. Engine off and cool. Jack the front of the car and support it on stands so the wheels are off the ground, you'll need to turn the steering freely.
2. Place a drain pan under the reservoir area and lay rags down, this job makes a mess no matter how careful you are.

### Draining
1. Siphon as much old fluid out of the reservoir as you can with a turkey baster or fluid extractor.
2. For a more complete flush, disconnect the return line into a drain pan and cap the reservoir outlet so it doesn't siphon the new fluid straight back out.
3. With the return line draining, have someone turn the steering lock to lock slowly while you keep topping the reservoir up, never let it run dry or you'll pump air into the system.

### Refilling and bleeding
1. Reconnect the return line and fill the reservoir with the correct spec fluid.
2. With the engine off and wheels off the ground, turn the steering slowly lock to lock several times to work air out, topping up as the level drops.
3. Start the engine briefly and repeat the lock-to-lock cycles, checking the level each time.
4. Lower the car, check for leaks at the return line connection, and check the level once the system is at operating temperature.

## Torque Specs
Return line clamp or fitting: verify against RealOEM or dealer spec. These are low-torque fittings, overtightening a hose clamp here damages the hose.

## Part Numbers
Power steering fluid: BMW specifies a particular fluid for this system (commonly a CHF-type hydraulic fluid rather than conventional ATF on this generation), verify the correct spec against your owner's manual or RealOEM.com before buying. Using the wrong fluid attacks the seals.

## Tools
Fluid extractor or turkey baster, drain pan, jack and stands, plenty of rags, a second person helps a lot for the lock-to-lock cycling.

## Difficulty
Medium

## Time Estimate
1-1.5 hours

## Notes
Never let the reservoir run dry while cycling the steering, that pumps air deep into the system and turns a simple fluid change into a long bleeding session. Whining from the pump after the job almost always means trapped air, not a failed pump, cycle it again before assuming the worst. Use only the BMW-specified fluid, this is one of those places where generic is actively harmful rather than just suboptimal.
