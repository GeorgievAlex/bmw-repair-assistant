# E90 chassis overview

E90 is the sedan body code for the 2005-2011 BMW 3 Series generation. The same platform also covers the E91 Touring (wagon), E92 (coupe), and E93 (convertible).

Rear-wheel drive unless the car is badged xi, which means xDrive all-wheel drive. Front suspension is a double-pivot MacPherson strut design, rear is a multi-link axle.

The US-market 325i with the N52B25 engine was sold through roughly the 2006-2008 model years, before the LCI (facelift) update changed the front and rear lights, bumpers, and some interior trim.
